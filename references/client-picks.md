# Client picks

Details the client has said they love. Reuse them where a new piece fits, and don't remove them in a check pass. Add each new pick with the client's own words and where it lives in the code or files.

## 1. Wrapping-cloth leather pad
From an earlier leather seat the client loved: a pad made like a Korean wrapping cloth rather than a tube with straps. Six uneven trapezoid panels split by slanted seams with a fine sunk seam between them, tones black, washed charcoal and washed indigo with no two neighbours matching, each end gathered into a knot with two flat cloth ears running out to soft points, deliberately asymmetric, and a glossier leather tether closing on a cast toggle. A strong starting point for a bag body or flap.

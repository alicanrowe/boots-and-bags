# Construction

Pick the construction before drawing panels: it sets the lasting allowance, the sole, and what can be made by hand.

## Boots
| Construction | How the upper meets the sole | By hand? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cemented | Upper lasted under the insole and glued; sole glued on | Yes, easiest | Good first pair. Not resoleable by most cobblers. |
| Stitchdown | Upper turned outward and stitched through a midsole | Yes | Visible flange; flexible; resoleable. |
| Blake / Blake-rapid | Stitched straight through insole and sole | Needs a machine | Sleek edge; less water resistant. |
| Hand-welted | Welt sewn to an insole holdfast by hand, then sole to welt | Yes, hard | The bespoke standard; learn it on a course. |
| Goodyear welted | Same idea, machine sewn to a canvas rib | Factory or workshop | Resoleable; usual for heavy boots. |

Leather starting points (confirm with the supplier and on the mockup):
- Boot uppers: calf 1.2 to 1.6 mm; heavier work/engineer boots 1.8 to 2.4 mm.
- Linings: calf or goat 0.8 to 1.0 mm.
- Counters and toe stiffeners: added between upper and lining; they change the outside shape, so model them.

## Bags
| Construction | Edges | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Raw edge, saddle stitched | Edges sanded, burnished or painted | Veg-tan or firm leather; hand-stitch friendly. |
| Turned (inside-out) | Seams hidden inside | Softer leather; needs a turning allowance and room to turn. |
| Folded edge | Edge folded over and glued before stitching | Neat, needs skiving. |
| Bound edge | Edge wrapped in a binding strip | Hides layered edges. |

## Who makes it
- **By hand:** cemented or stitchdown boots, any bag. Pattern sheets, cutting list and hardware are enough.
- **Bootmaker or small workshop:** welted boots from our patterns, last and hardware. Send a tech pack: renders, patterns, cutting list, leathers, construction, sole, hardware files.
- **Sampling factory:** samples, then runs usually from about 50 pairs per style. Same tech pack plus a size range.
Contacting any maker or ordering anything waits for the client's go-ahead.

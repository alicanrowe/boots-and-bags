# Hardware

Cast or printed metal parts follow the casting floors in the sculpted-metal-casting skill (0.8 to 1.0 mm minimum on any vein, wire or prong; one watertight body per part). On top of those:

## Fit to the leather
- Model every part against the real leather stack at that point (upper plus lining plus any reinforcement), not the bare surface.
- Eyelets and grommets: buy stock ones and a matching setter unless the eyelet itself is the design. A cast eyelet needs a post long enough for the stack plus 1.5 to 2 mm to roll.
- Speed hooks and rivets: post plus a back cap; hole in the leather 0.2 to 0.3 mm over the post.
- Buckles: inside width = strap width plus 1 to 2 mm. Tongue reaches the far bar. Strap holes on a 10 to 15 mm pitch.
- Heel plates and toe guards: screwed or nailed into the sole, so they need countersunk holes and enough metal round each (2 mm minimum).
- D-rings and strap anchors on bags: wire at least 3 mm for anything carrying weight, closed (cast as a ring, not an open hook).

## Wear
- Anything on a flex line (instep, ankle) must not dig in when the boot flexes 30 degrees.
- No sharp edge facing the wearer or a garment. Points and needles face outward and away from straps and laces.
- Hardware on the inside of the ankle clears the other boot by 10 mm when standing.

## Making it
Print in castable resin and cast (silver, brass, bronze), or direct-print metal through a service. Bought hardware goes on the cutting list with a part number.
